WHAT'S IN THE HOUSE?

homie is three open AI models living together on your Mac: ears, a brain and a voice. here's who they are, what they do, and why none of them ever phones a data center.

ONE SENTENCE.

watch it go from your mouth to done, without leaving the house

sent to the internet: 0 bytes
  1. 01the ears

    you talk. homie's ears turn your voice into words, live, while you're still talking. the audio never leaves the Mac.

  2. 02the brain

    the brain reads the words and decides what to do. it writes the plan out piece by piece, about 15 little chunks a second.

  3. 03the hands

    the plan becomes real actions on your Mac: a pasta timer starts, the lights go down. two jobs from one sentence.

  4. 04the voice

    the voice says it back in a real human-sounding voice, made on your Mac's Neural Engine in about a tenth of a second.

HOW BIG
IS A BRAIN?

every dot is a million tiny numbers. that's how a brain like this remembers language.

homie's brain has 4,000,000,000 of them.

we squish each one down to 4 bits, so the whole thing packs into 2.7 GB. smaller than a few movies.

the big cloud brains are hundreds of times bigger. they need a warehouse. homie needs your Mac.
