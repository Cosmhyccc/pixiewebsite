Pixie privacy

Updated October 22, 2026.

Support: Message the devs

Information used to provide Pixie

Pixie stores your account email, sign-in credentials securely through Supabase Auth, onboarding choices, subscription status and feature usage. The Mac app stores its sign-in session in macOS Keychain. Stripe processes payments; Pixie does not receive or store your full card number.

When you use AI features, your voice, dictated text, requested screen snapshots and relevant task context are sent to Pixie’s backend and OpenAI to process your request. Microphone, screen and computer-control access are governed by your macOS permissions. You can revoke these in System Settings.

Product analytics

Pixie uses Mixpanel to measure app interactions, onboarding progress, errors and timing. Analytics events do not include the name you type, voice recordings, dictated text, screen images, passwords or payment-card details. You can turn analytics off in Pixie’s menu.

Service providers and retention

Supabase provides account and backend infrastructure; OpenAI provides AI processing; Stripe handles billing; Mixpanel provides product analytics. These services may process data outside your country. Account and subscription records are retained while needed to provide the service, resolve support requests, prevent abuse and meet legal obligations. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion; required billing records may need to be retained.

Your choices

You can sign out, disable analytics, revoke macOS permissions, manage your subscription from Pixie’s Account menu, or contact us to delete your account. Pixie does not sell your personal information.
